This Skill must not write a second art direction or a second runtime.

## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one cut-paper totem, not a reduced illustration. Shape comes first: a bold contour, a few geometric blocks and a white cut edge. Keep screenprint grain, uneven ink and paper fiber. Color is lively and limited, remixed from the photograph: coral, warm yellow, plant green, mist blue, cream, brick. One symbol, stable, in a large field. Type, if any, is a tiny handmade brand word. Reject texture overload, a commercial cartoon, gray mud and candy color.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-305/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
